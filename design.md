# Course Creator Japan Landing Design

## Purpose

This repository is for a Japan-market landing page that tests whether Course Creator can be positioned as a B2B workflow solution for AI/DX training delivery.

The landing page should not sell "AI-generated slides." It should sell a concrete business outcome:

> Turn AI/DX training requirements into teachable, reviewable, auditable, and report-ready training packages.

Course Creator remains the production engine behind the offer. The public-facing product should be framed around reducing training delivery labor, review risk, and reporting work.

## Market Hypothesis

Japan has visible demand signals for AI/DX training, reskilling, and qualification preparation:

- Yano Research estimates Japan's commercial corporate training service market at JPY 575.8 billion for FY2024 and forecasts JPY 613.0 billion for FY2025.
  Source: https://www.yanoresearch.com/press/press.php/3895
- Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare runs Human Resource Development Support Grants for employers that conduct planned vocational training for workers.
  Source: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/koyou/kyufukin/d01-1.html
- JDLA reports cumulative G検定 examinees of 202,215 after G2026#2.
  Source: https://www.jdla.org/certificate/
- GUGA reported 28,415 examinees for the 2026 February 生成AIパスポート exam and 83,041 cumulative examinees at that point.
  Source: https://guga.or.jp/2026-03-16

These signals do not prove product demand. They only suggest that AI/DX training has budget, institutional attention, and repeated delivery needs.

## Core Positioning

### Internal Positioning

Course Creator is a spec-to-delivery workflow engine:

1. Parse training requirements, certification syllabi, policies, or internal documents.
2. Decompose them into modules, lessons, learning goals, and assessment points.
3. Generate structured training content, slides, diagrams, exercises, and instructor notes.
4. Run quality checks and create review-ready artifacts.
5. Collect human expert review and produce audit/reporting evidence.
6. Package final materials for training delivery and project reporting.

### External Positioning

Use buyer language:

> AI/DX training delivery package for organizations that need course materials, instructor support, review evidence, and completion reports without building everything manually.

Japanese-facing draft:

> AI/DX研修を、教材・講師用資料・確認テスト・レビュー記録・成果報告まで含めた実施可能な研修パッケージに整えます。

## Primary Buyer Segments

### Segment 1: Training Providers

Examples:

- Corporate training companies
- DX training vendors
- Certification prep providers
- Associations that run member training

Pain:

- Need to launch AI/DX courses quickly.
- Need to customize material by industry or client.
- Need to keep instructor delivery consistent.
- Need to reduce content editing and slide production labor.

Likely offer:

> AI/DX研修パッケージ制作支援

### Segment 2: Universities and Continuing Education Units

Examples:

- University extension centers
- Professional education units
- Career development centers
- Research centers running public training

Pain:

- Need AI/DX courses aligned with policy, industry demand, or certification goals.
- Need credible expert review.
- Need report-ready deliverables for internal approval or external funding.

Likely offer:

> AI/DX人材育成講座 立ち上げ支援

### Segment 3: Organizations Using Reskilling or Training Budgets

Examples:

- SMEs implementing AI tools
- HR/L&D departments
- Digital transformation offices
- Compliance, security, or AI governance teams

Pain:

- Internal AI policies and documents are not training-ready.
- Need employee training, tests, records, and follow-up reports.
- Need to show training evidence for internal governance or subsidies.

Likely offer:

> 生成AI活用・AIガバナンス研修パッケージ

## Jobs To Be Done

The landing page should focus on these jobs:

1. "We need to launch an AI/DX training program quickly."
2. "We have source documents and policies, but no training package."
3. "We need instructor materials, exercises, and tests."
4. "We need human review records before delivering AI-generated content."
5. "We need a completion report or review report for stakeholders."

Avoid leading with:

- "Generate many slides."
- "Replace instructors."
- "Automatic course creation."
- "Cheaper content."

Those messages are easy to dismiss and invite comparison with generic AI tools.

## Productized Offers

### Offer A: AI/DX Training Launch Package

Target:

- Training providers
- University extension units
- Associations

Deliverables:

- Training map and module outline
- Instructor slides
- Learner handouts
- Exercises and answer explanations
- Instructor guide
- Syllabus/objective alignment table
- Review summary
- Delivery report template

Estimated price test:

- JPY 1.5M to 2.5M for a focused launch package
- JPY 3.0M to 5.0M for co-development with customization and review

> 30分相談を予約する

### Offer B: Generative AI Governance Training Package

- Legal/compliance
- DX offices

Deliverables:

- Training content from internal AI-use policy
- Scenario-based exercises
- Knowledge check
- Instructor notes

Estimated price test:

- JPY 1.0M to 3.0M depending on document scope and review requirements

Landing CTA:

> 社内AI研修の相談をする

Target:

- JPY 300K to 1.0M per project as add-on

Landing CTA:

> 教材レビューの流れを確認する

## Landing Page Goal

The first landing page should not try to close online purchase. It should create qualified conversations.

Primary conversion:

- Book a 30-minute consultation.

Secondary conversion:

- Download a sample delivery package.
- Request a sample review report.

Qualification fields:

- Organization type
- Training topic
- Expected launch timing
- Existing source material
- Need for review/reporting
- Budget range

## Key Proof Assets Needed

The landing page should eventually include:

- Sample training map
- Sample instructor slide
- Sample learner handout
- Sample review summary
- Sample delivery report
- Before/after workflow diagram
- Short case note based on iPAS AI materials

Do not overclaim official recognition, exam pass rates, or certification endorsement.

## Strategic Bet

The Japan opportunity is not "Japanese iPAS." It is:

> AI/DX training operations for organizations that must repeatedly transform requirements, documents, and expert knowledge into deliverable training packages.

iPAS-style certification content can be a demonstration pattern, but the larger market is corporate training, reskilling delivery, AI governance training, and training-provider production workflows.

## Validation Plan

### 30-Day Test

1. Build a focused landing page with one primary offer.
2. Prepare a sample AI/DX training delivery package.
3. Contact 20 potential Japanese partners or buyers:
   - Training companies
   - DX consultants
   - Certification prep providers
   - University continuing education units
   - Taiwan-Japan business/education connectors
4. Ask whether they would pay for:
   - Fast AI/DX training package production
   - Industry customization
   - Instructor materials
   - Review records
   - Completion/report templates
5. Measure:
   - Consultation bookings
   - Requests for sample package
   - Budget range
   - Whether they already have training delivery pressure
   - Whether they would partner, buy, or only express interest

### Kill Criteria

Pause the Japan landing push if:

- Fewer than 3 of 20 target contacts agree to a follow-up conversation.
- Buyers only want free samples or one-off slide generation.
- No buyer can name a budget owner.
- Review/reporting is treated as nice-to-have rather than a paid deliverable.

### Proceed Criteria

Proceed if:

- At least 3 buyers ask for a proposal.
- At least 1 buyer has a training launch or proposal deadline within 90 days.
- At least 1 buyer can discuss a package above JPY 1M.
- Review/reporting is explicitly valued as part of delivery.
